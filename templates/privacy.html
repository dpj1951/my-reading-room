Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Digbe eSolutions LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates My Reading Alcove. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: your email address, created when you sign up.

Book library data: titles, authors, ISBNs, ratings, summaries, read dates, cover images, and any other information you enter into the app.

Payment information: if you subscribe, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number — Stripe handles all payment data under their own privacy policy.

Usage data: server logs may include your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We use this only for debugging and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the My Reading Alcove service
  • To authenticate your account and keep your library secure
  • To process subscription payments via Stripe
  • To respond to support requests sent to support@myreadingalcove.com
  • To send transactional emails (password reset, subscription confirmations)

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase's PostgreSQL database. We use row-level security to ensure each user can only access their own data. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never stored in plain text. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and library data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your data within 30 days. You can export your library at any time using the Export CSV feature before requesting deletion.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Export your book library via the built-in CSV export
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@myreadingalcove.com.

7. Children's Privacy

My Reading Alcove is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or an in-app notice. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy? Contact us at support@myreadingalcove.com.
